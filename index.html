FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about Araha.

Is Araha free to use?
Yes, Araha is completely free. Fare alerts, flight tracking, and boarding status updates cost you nothing — just standard WhatsApp data charges apply.
Where does the fare data come from?
Araha pulls real-time fare data from Google Flights, covering Air Peace, Arik Air, Ibom Air, and other Nigerian carriers. A built-in safety filter ensures only verified Nigerian airlines appear in results.
How do boarding status updates work?
Passengers at the airport report what they see (e.g. "boarding now gate 12"). Once 2+ independent passengers confirm the same status, it's broadcast to everyone tracking that flight. Conflicting reports are flagged as disputed and never pushed without verification.
Which airlines and routes are supported?
All major Nigerian domestic routes: Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Benin City, Kano, Calabar, Ilorin, Owerri, and Akure. We track Air Peace, Arik Air, Ibom Air, United Nigeria, Green Africa, ValueJet, Overland, NG Eagle, Max Air, Umza Air, and Enugu Air.
Do I need to install an app?
No. Araha works entirely on WhatsApp — the app you already use every day. Just send a message to get started. No downloads, no accounts, no passwords.
Can I set a target price?
Yes! When you subscribe, add your target price — e.g. SUBSCRIBE LOS ABV 80000. You'll only get an alert when the fare drops below ₦80,000.
